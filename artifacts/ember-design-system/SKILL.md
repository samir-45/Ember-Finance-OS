# Ember Design System usage notes

## Visual direction

Ember is a premium, calm personal-finance interface with a near-black foundation, a slow crimson/orange edge glow, subtle grain, and translucent glass surfaces. The center of the interface remains dark and readable.

## Color and hierarchy

- Use ember red for primary actions, active states, key chart series, and focused alerts.
- Keep red and orange off large page surfaces. Income is soft green or white; expenses use ember red.
- Use muted neutrals for secondary information and make all currency values easy to scan.
- Keep all amounts tabular; labels stay small and subdued without sacrificing contrast.

## Surfaces and motion

- Cards use a 16px radius, a 4–7% translucent white surface, a fine white border and top highlight, 24px blur, and a faint red-tinted shadow.
- Reserve stronger glow for hover or focus. Avoid pill-shaped card treatments.
- Use a slow atmospheric drift and restrained entrance/hover transitions. Honor reduced-motion preferences.

## Voice and content

- Explain financial information plainly; show the conversion basis whenever totals are converted.
- Use specific labels and concrete next actions rather than generic financial advice.

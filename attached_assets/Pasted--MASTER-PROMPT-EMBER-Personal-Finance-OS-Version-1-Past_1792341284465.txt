## 1. ROLE AND GOAL

You are a senior full-stack engineer and product designer. Build **Ember**, a production-grade personal finance web app for a freelancer who earns in **USD**, converts to **BDT** (Bangladeshi Taka), and spends in both currencies.

Ember tracks every income and expense, shows exactly where money goes (by category, sub-category, payment method, and subscription), and acts as a **financial coach** that tells the user what to improve next.

Build a complete, working, deployable V1. No placeholders, no fake "coming soon" buttons, no lorem ipsum. Seed realistic demo data so every screen looks alive on first load.

## 3. CORE CONCEPTS (DATA MODEL)

**Money rule:** store all amounts as integers in minor units (cents/poisha). Never use floats for money.

Tables:
- **users**: id, email, password_hash, base_currency (default BDT), created_at
- **accounts**: id, user_id, name, type (bank, wallet, cash, card, payoneer, paypal), currency (USD or BDT), opening_balance
- **categories**: id, user_id, name, parent_id (for sub-categories), type (income or expense), color, icon. Seed: Groceries (Vegetables, Meat and Fish, Snacks, Household), Rent, Utilities, Transport, Subscriptions, Dining, Health, Education, Family, Shopping, Savings, Investments, Taxes and Fees, Other
- **transactions**: id, user_id, account_id, category_id, type (income, expense, transfer), amount, currency, note, merchant, date, tags, payment_method, status (todo/planned, pending, done)
- **transfers**: id, from_account_id, to_account_id, amount_out, amount_in, exchange_rate (auto-calculated as amount_in / amount_out), fee, date. This is how a USD to BDT conversion is recorded, so the REAL rate the user got is always preserved.
- **subscriptions**: id, name, amount, currency, billing_cycle, next_billing_date, category_id, account_id, active
- **budgets**: id, category_id, month, limit_amount
- **goals**: id, name, target_amount, current_amount, deadline
- **exchange_rates**: date, from, to, rate (used only for converting totals when no transfer rate applies)
- **coach_insights**: id, user_id, created_at, type, severity, title, body, action

## 4. MULTI-CURRENCY LOGIC (IMPORTANT)

1. Each account has one currency. A USD client payment is **income** into a USD account.
2. Converting to BDT is a **transfer** (USD account to BDT account) with both amounts entered; the exchange rate is derived and stored.
3. Expenses are recorded from the account actually used, in that account's currency.
4. Dashboard has a currency toggle: **BDT / USD / Original**. Totals convert using the most recent real transfer rate; fall back to the `exchange_rates` table if none exists.
5. Always show a small label like "converted at 1 USD = 121.5 BDT" so conversions are never a mystery.
6. Add a "Conversion Tracker" card: average rate achieved, best rate, worst rate, total fees paid, so the user sees what conversions cost them.

## 5. SCREENS AND FEATURES

### 5.1 Command Center (main dashboard, Kanban style)
A board of draggable glass cards arranged in columns. The user can rearrange cards; save layout per user.

Top strip (KPI cards): Net worth, This month income, This month spent, Savings rate %, Runway (months of expenses covered by current balances).

Columns / widgets:
- **Cash Flow**: income vs expense, monthly area chart
- **Where It Went**: donut chart by category with percentages, click a slice to drill into sub-categories (e.g., Groceries to Vegetables vs Meat and Fish vs Snacks)
- **Accounts**: each account with balance and currency
- **Payment Methods**: spending split by account/method
- **Subscriptions**: list with monthly total, next billing dates, "total per year" warning
- **Budgets**: progress bars with color shifts (calm to amber to red as limit approaches)
- **Goals**: progress rings
- **Coach**: latest 3 insights (see 5.4)
- **Recent Activity**: latest transactions

### 5.2 Transactions Board (Kanban)
Columns: **Planned → Pending → Done**. Cards are transactions; dragging between columns updates status. Filters by date, category, account, currency, tag. Search. Bulk edit. CSV import and export.

### 5.3 Quick Add
Floating "+" button, and keyboard shortcut **N**. A fast form: amount, currency, category, account, note, date. Support natural-language entry: typing `groceries 1200 bkash` or `client payment 800 usd payoneer` auto-fills the fields (parse with the AI API, with a simple regex fallback). Include a dedicated **Convert Currency** quick action that creates the transfer.

### 5.4 Financial Coach (the smart part)
Two layers:

**Layer 1: Rules engine (deterministic, always works, no AI needed).** Run after every change and daily:
- 50/30/20 check: needs / wants / savings share of income, compared to targets
- Savings rate below 20% triggers a warning
- Category spike: any category more than 30% above its 3-month average
- Subscription creep: subscriptions above 5% of income, or unused/duplicate-looking ones
- Emergency fund: balances vs 3 to 6 months of expenses
- Income volatility: freelance income varies, so suggest a "buffer month" rule (pay yourself a fixed salary from a buffer)
- Conversion tip: flag when conversions happened at a rate well below the user's average
- Fee drag: total fees paid this quarter

**Layer 2: AI explanation.** Send only anonymized monthly aggregates (never raw notes or merchant names unless the user turns on a setting) to the configured AI provider and ask for a short, plain-language monthly review: what went well, what to fix, and the single most important next action. Show it as a card with a "Next Focus" highlight. Cache results; regenerate monthly or on demand. If the API is unavailable, show the rules-engine insights only.

Each insight has: severity (info/warning/critical), a plain-language title, one-sentence reason, and one concrete action button (e.g., "Set a Dining budget of 8,000 BDT").

Add a **Money Health Score** (0 to 100) built from savings rate, budget adherence, emergency fund, and subscription load, shown as a glowing ring.

### 5.5 Reports
Monthly and yearly report pages: income, expenses, top categories, month-over-month change, subscription total, conversion summary. Export as PDF and CSV.

### 5.6 Settings
Profile, base currency, categories manager, accounts manager, budgets, data export, delete account, AI coach on/off, and privacy toggle.

## 6. UI / DESIGN SYSTEM (VERY IMPORTANT)

**Mood:** premium, dark, cinematic, calm and minimal despite the fire. Think Apple visionOS glass meets a molten ember glow.

**Background:**
- Base: near-black (#050505) with subtle film-grain noise overlay.
- Hyper-realistic fiery gradient: layered, blurred radial gradients in deep crimson (#7a0a0a), ember red (#ff3b1f), and a touch of burnt orange (#ff6a2b), placed low and in the corners like glowing embers behind the interface. Very slow animated drift (60s loops, CSS/Canvas, GPU-friendly). The center stays dark so content remains readable.
- Optional: tiny floating ember particles (canvas), kept subtle, disabled when `prefers-reduced-motion` is on.

**Glass cards:**
- Background rgba(255,255,255,0.04 to 0.07), `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(140%)`
- 1px border rgba(255,255,255,0.10), with a faint top-edge highlight (inner glow)
- Corner radius **16px** (softly rounded, not pill-shaped, not sharp)
- Soft shadow with a very faint red tint
- Hover: slight lift, border brightens, subtle red glow

**Typography:** Inter or Geist; large light-weight numbers for money, small uppercase muted labels; tabular numerals for all amounts.

**Color use:** mostly black, white, and gray. Red/orange only as accent: primary buttons, active states, key chart series, alerts. Income is a calm soft green or white, expenses are ember red. Don't overuse color.

**Motion:** Framer Motion; numbers count up on load, cards fade/slide in with a stagger, drag-and-drop feels physical. Keep it smooth (60fps).

**Layout:** left slim icon sidebar (glass), top bar with currency toggle and quick add, a responsive grid. Fully responsive: on mobile, cards stack and the sidebar becomes a bottom tab bar.

**Details:** skeleton loaders in glass style, empty states with a short helpful message, accessible contrast (WCAG AA for text), full keyboard support, dark mode only for V1.
